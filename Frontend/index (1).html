How NYAYA-SAHAY Works

End-to-end legal document pipeline connecting Police Officers, Legal Reviewers, Citizens, and Administrators.

Step 1
👮 Police Officer

Upload & Lock Document

Police officers upload FIRs and case papers. Files are cryptographically locked and stored safely into the Document Vault.

Step 2
⚖️ Legal Reviewer

Read & Extract Key Details

Legal reviewers review documents, fix mistakes, map BNS/IPC sections, and generate structured summaries for the Case Details List.

Step 3
👤 Citizen / Public

Search & Inquiry

Citizens ask questions or search case status. Matching files show instant public summaries, reports, and search memory.

Step 4
🛡️ System Admin

Access Control & Audit

Administrators manage permission rules ("Who Can See What"), track file versions, and maintain complete Activity Logs.

Core Institutional Capabilities

Key pillars powering modern legal document verification and criminal investigation management

AI Document Intelligence

Automated OCR, BNS/IPC legal section mapping, key entity extraction, and instant case summary generation.

Tamper Verification

Cryptographic SHA-256 evidence hashing for court admissibility, verifying document integrity instantly.

Role-Based Authorization

Granular access policies tailored for Police Officers, Legal Reviewers, Citizens, and Administrators.

Immutable Audit Trail

Complete event telemetry recording every document view, download, edit, and access attempt.
